Research groups

Lab notebook, literature with DOIs, meetings whose actions become tasks, and a supervisor who can watch without editing.

Student engineering teams

Sub-teams, parts and orders, budget lines with receipts, and a weekly summary for the group chat. Free for students.

Engineering companies, 20 to 200 people

Roles and permissions, approvals for spending and deletions, requirements traced to tasks, GitHub linked to the work.

One board for the work, the people and the money.

People × weeks

Everyone’s work for each week on one board, with Kanban and Gantt views of the same tasks.

Parts and budgets

Components with suppliers and order status, budget lines, spending with receipts and a burn-rate forecast.

Sign-offs and approvals

Workflow stages with checklists. Leads sign off reviews; admins approve purchases and deletions.

Requirements and risks

Requirements linked to the tasks that deliver them, and a risk register scored by likelihood and impact.

GitHub, Drive and Calendar

Pull requests and issues link to tasks by reference. Files stay in your own Drive or OneDrive.

Live for the whole team

Changes appear for everyone within a second. See who is online and what they are looking at.
