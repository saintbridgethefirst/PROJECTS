Plan the work. Prove it was done.

Project management for teams that build hardware. Tasks, people, parts, budgets and sign-offs on one live board, with an audit trail that only the server can write.

Built for engineering programmes, not to-do lists.

Audit trail

Every change is recorded by the server and sealed to the one before it. Check it in your browser; export it for your auditor.

Sign-offs and stage gates

Design reviews with checklists. Leads sign off before work moves to the next stage.

Parts, orders and budgets

Suppliers and order status, approvals for purchases over a limit, spending against budget lines.

Requirements and risks

Requirements traced to the tasks that deliver and verify them. Risks scored by likelihood and impact.

People and capacity

Hours, skills and time off for each person, and their workload across every project.

One live board

Weeks, Kanban and Gantt over the same tasks. Changes reach the whole team within a second.
