How work moves

From plan to proof, on one record.

  1. PlanBreak the programme into weeks, owners and stages.
  2. BuildTrack parts, orders and hours as work happens.
  3. ReviewRun design reviews against a checklist.
  4. Sign offThe responsible lead approves the stage gate.
  5. RecordThe server seals every step into the audit trail.

Audit trail

A record nobody can quietly edit.

Every change to tasks, parts, budgets, members and roles is written by the server, numbered and sealed to the entry before it. Not even the team owner can change or remove an entry.

  • Who, when, and each field before and after
  • Checked in your browser: a gap or an edit is named
  • CSV export for your quality manager or auditor
