📘 The field manual

Your team’s whole week, in one picture.

Trawpie (Technical Research And Welfare for People In Education) is a shared board of people × weeks. Everyone can see who is doing what, what’s late and what’s next, and it updates live for the whole team. It’s built for universities (student teams, research groups, and students planning a dissertation or thesis) and for engineering teams and organisations. This page shows you everything it can do, one bite at a time.

Who it’s for

The same board works for a student writing a thesis and for a company running a product launch. Switch on the tools you need, and leave the rest off.

🎓

Universities and research

Free for students and university staff with Pro Education.

  • Dissertations and theses: undergraduate, master’s and PhD. A plan from your hand-in date, chapter word counts, a proposal helper and feedback on drafts from your supervisor. How it works ↓
  • Research groups and labs: a lab notebook on every task, a reading list with DOIs and BibTeX, meeting notes whose actions become tasks, and requirements and risks.
  • Student teams and societies: Formula Student, drone, rocketry and robotics teams, with sub-teams, parts, budget and design sign-offs.
Plan a dissertationFormula StudentStudent team demo
🏢

Teams and organisations

For engineering companies, R&D programmes and departments with many projects.

  • Stage gates and sign-offs: your own workflow (Design → Review → Build → Test), with who must sign each stage off and a checklist.
  • Money and suppliers: parts and orders, purchase approvals over a limit you set, budget lines and a burn-rate forecast.
  • Reporting: requirements, a risk register, time logging, and a printable progress report for a manager, customer or funder.
  • People from outside: invite clients, suppliers and contractors as Guests. They work on the tasks you give them and comment, and nothing else.

Checklists · Supervisor · 📚 Literature

👩‍🏫 For supervisors

Invite your supervisor during setup, or later from the Supervisor tab. They join with the Supervisor role: they see the plan, chapters and progress and can comment, but can’t change your plan. Supervising several students? Each student invites them to their own workspace, and all of them appear on the supervisor’s home page.

  1. Send a draftThe student sends a chapter with a link to the file, a note and a date for feedback (two weeks by default).
  2. A review taskIt lands in the supervisor’s row on the board, with the file attached and the student’s note as a comment.
  3. Approve or ask for changesThe supervisor opens Chapters, reads the draft and decides. Asking for changes needs a short note.
  4. The student sees itThe feedback arrives as a comment that mentions the student, and the chapter shows Approved or Changes asked.

03

Weeks that match your real calendar

Pick the day your project starts and every week follows on, 7 days apart. Change one week’s date and all the weeks after it move with it. Number them your way, and give each week a headline number with the weekly metric.

Try it: your weeks

✏️ Click a metric badge to type your own, e.g. “Flights: 3”.

📆

Set the dates once

Right-click the board ▸ Set week dates… and choose when Week 1 starts. Every later week lines up behind it, and new weeks you add continue the run.

↪️

Change one, the rest follow

Right-click a week ▸ Set start date…. That week and all the weeks after it shift together, 7 days apart. Earlier weeks stay put. Ctrl + Z undoes it.

🗓️

Own numbering or calendar weeks

Settings ▸ General ▸ Week numbering: count from Week 1, or use the calendar week of the year (Week 43 for the week of 19 October). You can still give any week its own name, like “Reading week”.

🎯

Weekly metric

A small badge on every week header for the one number that matters that week: hours logged, budget left, flights flown, words written. Click it to type. Rename it in Settings ▸ Fields, or hide it.

📍

Always know where “now” is

The week containing today is marked THIS WEEK, and 🎯 Today scrolls straight to it. Dated weeks also power the timeline, the burndown and calendar exports.

Questions people ask

Someone didn’t get the invitation email

Ask them to check spam. In Settings ▸ Members ▸ Pending invitations you can see whether it was emailed, press Resend, or Copy link to send it yourself. They must sign in with exactly the email address you invited.

Does everyone see my changes straight away?

Yes. Edits save automatically and appear for everyone in the team within a second, with no save button and no refresh.

I deleted something by mistake

Press Ctrl + Z straight away to undo. For finance, deleted budget lines can be restored from the finance history. Owners can also download a full backup any time from Team data.

Where are my files stored?

In your own Google Drive or OneDrive. Trawpie only keeps a link on the task, so nothing is copied to our servers.

Can I use my university OneDrive?

Yes. Upload or find the file in OneDrive, click Share ▸ Copy link, and paste it into Attach a file. Trawpie names the file from the link and shows it as a OneDrive file. Your university doesn’t need to approve anything for this.

How do I get Pro Education?

Open Settings ▸ Plan. If you signed in with a university email (.ac.uk, .edu…), just switch it on. If you use a personal email, add your university email there, enter the 6-digit code we send, and you’re done. The team’s owner switches it on for the team.

Can supervisors or sponsors look without editing?

Yes. Invite them with the Viewer role. They can see the board, analytics and timeline but can’t change anything. For a dissertation, use the Supervisor role instead: supervisors can also comment and give feedback on chapter drafts.

Can I use Trawpie on my own, for a dissertation or thesis?

Yes. A team can be just you (plus your supervisor, if you like). Create a team and choose Start with ▸ Dissertation or thesis. It’s free, and your home page also has a personal Today view for habits and tasks outside your projects.

Can a company or department use it?

Yes. Each project is a team with its own plan, roles and data. Turn on workflows and sign-offs, purchase approvals, requirements and risks in Settings ▸ Features, and invite clients or contractors as Guests. The Organisation plan puts many teams on one bill.

Can I bring in work from Excel, Jira or Trello?

Yes. Go to Team data ▸ Import tasks and choose an Excel file, a CSV, a Jira export or a Trello board export. You get a preview before anything is added.

Is my data private?

Only members of your team can see your team’s work, and the server enforces this. How it’s protected, backed up and what’s still to come is on the security page; the privacy policy has the details.
